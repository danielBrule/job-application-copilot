Review and rewrite the supplied French CV draft against the supplied final English CV.

Goal

Produce the final French CV in natural, concise and professional fr-FR, suitable for the French recruitment market. The final English CV is the sole factual authority.

Perform two silent review passes before returning the result.

Pass 1 — Factual fidelity

Compare every section, skill entry, experience block and bullet with the final English CV.

Correct any discrepancy involving:

- employers, roles and professional relationships;
- dates, numbers, currencies and metrics;
- technologies, technical scope and outcomes;
- responsibilities, contribution and ownership;
- seniority, positioning and evidence strength;
- omitted, added, strengthened or weakened information.

Do not introduce evidence that is absent from the final English CV. Do not infer facts from French style references.

Preserve distinctions such as shared ownership, supporting contribution, prototypes, self-directed work and adjacent experience. Never convert them into sole ownership or production experience.

Pass 2 — French CV quality

Rewrite wording that sounds translated from English.

Make the French:

- natural, concise and idiomatic;
- appropriate for the seniority established by the English source;
- suitable for a two-page professional CV;
- consistent in terminology, tense and tone;
- free from awkward syntax, unnecessary repetition and unnecessarily long sentences.

Replace unnecessary anglicisms while retaining established Data, AI, technology and consulting terminology when translation would sound artificial or change the meaning.

Preserve:

- the approved positioning and evidence hierarchy;
- the order and number of sections, skill entries, experience blocks and bullets;
- the factual meaning and ownership boundary of every statement;
- the approximate information density of the English CV.

Do not make the candidate sound more commercial, technical, strategic or senior than the English source supports.

Success criteria

Before returning the result, verify that:

- every factual claim is supported by the final English CV;
- every number, currency, metric, employer and role remains consistent;
- no evidence has been added, removed, strengthened or weakened;
- terminology is internally consistent;
- the French reads as an original professional CV rather than a translation.

Output

Return only the complete rewritten French CV content using the supplied structured-output schema.

Do not return a change log, review notes, explanations, readiness assessment, commentary or Markdown.
